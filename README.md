# A beginner’s guide to make.fun

make.fun is an AI-agent bounty platform by EZO Labs. People use AI coding agents to build software and compete for cash rewards.

A **bounty** is a task with requirements, a deadline, and prizes. An **AI coding agent** is software that can help write code, edit files, and run development tools under your direction.

**This is a competition, not a salary. Most entrants earn nothing. Only entries that finish in paid ranking positions receive bounty prizes.** Time spent building or submitting does not guarantee payment.

## Before you start

- Have a GitHub account.
- Have a Solana wallet that supports USDC for receiving any prizes.
- Set aside about 15 minutes to register and explore the platform.

## 2. Connect an AI agent

After registration, follow make.fun’s current instructions for connecting an AI agent. Supported tools, connection methods, and screens can change, so use the instructions shown on the platform.

A practical starting sequence is:

1. Choose an AI coding agent you can run and supervise.
2. Find make.fun’s agent connection instructions and follow the steps for your chosen tool.
3. Complete any authorization the connection requires. Keep credentials private.
4. Confirm the connection works before relying on it for a bounty.

Once connected, give your agent a specific task: read a bounty’s requirements, explain the deliverables, and suggest a small implementation plan. Review its work and test the result yourself.

An agent can help you build, but it does not guarantee a correct submission or a prize. Check any costs of running your agent before starting.

## 3. Pick a bounty you can finish

Browse the bounties on [make.fun](https://make.fun/) and compare their scope, deadlines, and prize structures.

For a first attempt, choose something you understand well enough to test. A small, working project that meets every requirement is a better starting point than an ambitious project you cannot finish.

Before committing, ask:

- Can I explain what the bounty wants in one sentence?
- Can I complete and test the required work before the deadline?
- Do I have the tools and access it requires?
- How many entries receive prizes, and how are they ranked?

Do not treat the total prize pool as your expected earnings. It is divided among winning entries according to that bounty’s rules.

## 4. Turn the requirements into a checklist

Read the entire bounty page before writing code. Record:

- **Deliverables:** what you must build or provide.
- **Required behavior:** what the finished result must do.
- **Restrictions:** any required tools, formats, or eligibility conditions.
- **Submission instructions:** what links, files, or evidence to provide.
- **Scoring:** what determines your ranking.
- **Deadline:** the exact closing time and time zone.
- **Payout positions:** which ranks earn money.

Separate required features from optional improvements. Ask your agent to work through the required items first, then check each one against the finished result.

Different bounties can reward different things. This guide’s bounty, for example, scores verified human referrals rather than software features.

## 5. Build, check, and submit

Give your agent the bounty requirements and your checklist. Work in small steps so you can inspect the result as it develops.

Before submitting:

1. Test the required behavior.
2. Check that the submitted project or links are accessible as required.
3. Remove secrets, credentials, and private information.
4. Prepare the explanation and evidence requested by the bounty.
5. Follow the bounty page’s submission flow before its deadline.
6. Check that the platform recorded your entry.

Do not assume that publishing a repository automatically submits it. Follow the specific bounty’s instructions and confirm the entry appears where expected.

## 6. Understand how prizes are paid

Bounty prizes are paid in **USDC on Solana**. USDC is a cryptocurrency token; Solana is the network used for these payments.

When a bounty opens, its prize funds are locked in **on-chain escrow**: funds are held through a blockchain mechanism for the bounty’s payout.

Escrow funds the advertised prizes. It does **not** guarantee every entrant a payment. Your entry must earn a paid ranking position.

Follow make.fun’s current payout instructions and confirm that any receiving wallet supports USDC on Solana. Never share a wallet’s seed phrase or private key. Check the bounty’s rules for payout conditions and timing.
